WHAT THE SCRIPT DOES
- 'Inspect': verifies the RSLinx driver and PLC without selecting or uploading.
- 'Select': selects the verified device in the standalone RSLinx browser.
- 'Upload': selects in standalone RSWho, then requires the Studio Who Active dialog already open and showing the correct path, verifies Upload button, and clicks Upload.
- The standalone RSWho selection does NOT automatically select the same PLC in Studio Who Active. This distinction is important.
- No Download, PLC write, project save, export, or automatic cleanup is implemented.
